# epistemic humility ai tools

Incorporate epistemic humility in AI troubleshooting tools by displaying rejections and unknowns.

Showing what was rejected or couldn't be determined in AI-generated reports builds trust over time, as opposed to presenting only confident answers.

**Why:** AI tools that hallucinate or omit uncertainties can mislead users. Transparency about limitations enhances reliability.

**How to apply:** Structure reports to include sections on rejections, gaps, and unknowns, and verify claims against source data.
