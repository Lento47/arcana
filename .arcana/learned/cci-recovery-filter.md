# cci recovery filter

CCI must be moving away from extreme, not deeper, to prevent catching falling knives.

**Why:** Entering when CCI is diverging from extremes reduces the risk of adverse continuation.
**How to apply:** Monitor CCI slope; require it to be positive for sells or negative for buys when CCI is extreme.
