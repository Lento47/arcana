# text gantt rendering

Gantt-style timelines can be rendered as aligned text bars with emoji markers

**Why:** A Gantt chart was needed to visualize Michael Jackson's life timeline. Instead of a graphical library, aligned monospace text bars with start-marker emojis (⛧, ▸) and colored-block emojis (██) effectively conveyed the timeline.

**How to apply:** Use monospace alignment with │ and ═ characters for borders. Place year labels on the left, emoji start markers, and ██ bars proportional to duration. Use emoji prefixes (🔵, 🟣) as color categories.
