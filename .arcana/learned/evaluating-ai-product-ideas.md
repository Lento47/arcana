# evaluating ai product ideas

Evaluate AI product ideas based on core thesis, epistemic humility, and anti-slop guards.

**Why:** When assessing whether an AI product will succeed, focus on how it handles hallucination, builds trust, and avoids generic outputs. These are critical factors for user adoption and long-term value.

**How to apply:** When reviewing an AI product idea, ask:
1. Does it have a sound core thesis? (e.g., grounding in verifiable sources)
2. Does it incorporate epistemic humility? (showing rejections, gaps, uncertainties)
3. Does it have mechanisms to avoid generic advice? (anti-slop guards)
