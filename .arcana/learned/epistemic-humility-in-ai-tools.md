# epistemic humility in ai tools

Show rejections, gaps, and uncertainties to build user trust.

**Why:** Presenting only confident answers without acknowledging limitations erodes trust. By showing what the AI rejected, what gaps exist, and what it couldn't determine, users get a transparent view of the AI's knowledge boundaries.

**How to apply:** Design AI outputs to include sections for rejections, gaps, and uncertainties. Structure the response as "here's what I found, here's what I threw out, here's what I don't know."
