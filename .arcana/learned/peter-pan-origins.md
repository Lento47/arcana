# peter pan origins

Peter Pan first appeared in 1902 before becoming a standalone play and novel.

**Why:** Understanding the evolution of a character across formats reveals how stories develop over time. **How to apply:** When building a narrative universe, consider introducing characters in smaller works before expanding them into full stories. Peter Pan appeared in *The Little White Bird* (1902), then the 1904 play *Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up*, and finally the 1911 novel *Peter and Wendy*.
