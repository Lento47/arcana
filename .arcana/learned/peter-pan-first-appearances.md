# peter pan first appearances

Peter Pan appeared in three distinct works across 12 years before becoming the iconic character

**Why:** Peter Pan evolved through multiple formats — a 1902 novel (*The Little White Bird*), a 1904 play (*Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up*), and a 1911 novel (*Peter and Wendy*). Each iteration expanded the character and world.

**How to apply:** When developing a character or story, consider how it might translate across different media and formats over time.
